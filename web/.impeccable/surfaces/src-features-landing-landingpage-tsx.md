# Public landing

Target: `src/features/landing/LandingPage.tsx`
Mode: Persuade. Scope: public `/` only; cabinet and Desktop unchanged.
The user delegates the visual decisions and asks to start building, rather than
present another selection round. Code-led: no image-generation tool is available.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An API connection expressed as a woven field. Refuse the small wordmark
over an empty ASCII background and the standard copy-plus-dashboard split hero.

OWN-WORLD: A pine-green field whose outer edges align with the existing
1328px reading column, visible header content, FAQ and footer without widening them,
pale green threads, warm neutral quiet
sections, locally served Golos Text, flat seams and softly raised controls. Green
owns the opening, not just its buttons. No imitation cloth textures or 3D objects.

STORY: Understand that Relay connects AI models to your apps; enter the cabinet
with optional help, or read four already-visible answers. No invented statistics.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact navigation above one wide green surface. A two-line
headline fills the upper region; brief API copy and Start sit beneath. Three
interlaced bundles cross the entire lower region, labelled application / Relay /
model. Constant-speed request strokes are the single authored motion; pause when
offscreen or hidden and remain still under reduced motion. On phones the strands
crop to their central crossing rather than shrinking into illegible decoration.

FORM: Grounded candidate 4, woven graphic systems; seed:6c2264b12379c6c6. The seven
candidates, in order, were type specimens, transit maps, film-program graphics,
woven graphics, record sleeves, gatefold publications, and contact sheets.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Challenger judgment

- Moon-shadow bazaar: declined; obscure product identification and clarity.
  Keep its discipline of giving a whole region to one colour, not its fantasy.
- Memory quilt: competitive on material coherence, weaker on product clarity;
  keep the assigned abstract weave rather than inventing personal histories.
- One-bit desktop: declined; wrongly suggests the local desktop product.
  Raise the assigned direction through clear control affordances.
- Teletext: declined; poor multilingual reading and unnecessary navigation.
  Raise the direction through legible, already-visible answers.
- Du Bois portraits: declined without real statistics; do not invent charts.
  Raise the direction through deliberate large-scale geometry.
- Midnight transit: competitive on clarity, more literal and familiar.
  Preserve its readable application-to-model sequence within the weave.

## Constraints

RU / EN / zh-CN, shared theme preference, guide and direct-entry links, existing
language picker behavior, keyboard and touch access, 320px layouts. Four FAQ
answers stay open. The diagram is illustrative, not a live activity indicator.
No backend, provider calls, customer data, deployment or desktop rebuild.
