# Design System: Relay Hosted Web

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Woven graphic systems"**

A connection is expressed through precise, interlaced line bundles. Pine green carries the public opening, workspace navigation and selected functional fields; warm paper and charcoal carry reading and operating surfaces. Golos Text and flat, quiet controls keep the geometry approachable. Hosted page bodies use compact joined summaries and task-shaped compositions; owner inventory and pool use Desktop-inspired cards adapted to Hosted controls and available source data. The marketing hero and moving traffic stay on the public landing.

This system covers the public `/` at `.landing.landing-home`, customer `/app/*` and owner `/admin/*` workspaces at `.relay-app.hosted-app`, their dialogs/help/empty states, and the separate `.relay-app.landing.hosted-entry` supporting entry. Desktop and backend behavior have separate owners. Surface specifications live in `.impeccable/surfaces/src-features-landing-landingpage-tsx.md` and `.impeccable/surfaces/src-features-app-hostedapp-tsx.md`.

**Key Characteristics:**
- A green field with exact, three-bundle vector geometry.
- Pine workspace navigation with one cream selected row.
- Warm light surfaces and neutral charcoal dark surroundings.
- Locally hosted Golos Text with separate display and operating type ramps.
- Constant-speed public request illustration; quiet account operations.
- Compact joined summaries, open request-token columns and responsive owner resource cards.

## Colors

Muted greens and cream threads sit against warm-neutral and charcoal surfaces; the frontmatter preserves the source's `light-dark()` pairs, resolved by each web root's selected `color-scheme`.

The sidecar's eight-step OKLCH ramps preview the light member's hue. The canonical `light-dark()` expressions define the shipped tokens for both themes.

### Primary
- **Connection green** (`primary`): wordmark strokes, FAQ action, header keyboard rings and the small FAQ weave mark.
- **Pine field** (`hero-ground`): the large hero region in both themes; use **cream ink**, **pale green secondary** and **muted green labels** inside it.
- **Mint, cream and sage threads**: the three geometric bundles; **request light** is reserved for the moving strokes.
- **Primary ink / hover**: theme-aware action contrast and interaction state, not additional brand accents.
- **Workspace pine / ink / secondary** (`pine`, `pine-ink`, `pine-secondary`): the green-family material for navigation and functional billing fields. The sidebar's navigation aliases reuse these values without changing the functional fields.
- **Navigation field, selection and brand** (`nav-bg`, `nav-active-bg`, `nav-active-ink`, `nav-brand`): pine background, cream selected row with fixed dark-pine text, and cream brand/focus color in both themes. Frontmatter records resolved colors: the source aliases background to `pine`, selected fill and brand to `pine-ink`.
- **Soft accent** (`accent-soft`): quiet selected tabs, pressed filter states and successful status backgrounds, not an extra raised layer.
- **Functional pine fields:** the balance panel uses `pine` / `pine-ink`, with secondary copy in `pine-secondary`. The balance action is cream with fixed dark-pine ink; its hover is the literal warm white `balance-action-hover` in both themes, not the theme-dependent surface token.

The balance panel contains its top-up form directly: amount input, four quick
amounts, configured payment-method selection and a cream submit button. Fields
use translucent pine-ink fills and pine-secondary labels; preset selection uses
the same cream/dark-pine pair as the primary action. Submission and validation
stay inline, with no amount or confirmation modal. Redundant captions under the
form and table are omitted; there is no shared preview disclosure footer.
Only historical operation details open a dialog.

### Neutral
- **Warm paper / charcoal** (`page`), **control surface** (`surface`) and **subtle state** (`subtle`) form the effective surroundings.
- **Reading ink** (`text`) and **secondary ink** (`secondary`) separate questions from answers without extra containers.
- **Seam** (`border`) divides answers and footer; **control edge** (`control-border`) outlines interactive controls.
- **Muted ink** (`muted`): supporting workspace labels. Error/error-soft, warning and info retain their existing semantic roles; chart series keep their distinct data colors rather than being repainted green.
- **Navigation ink and translucent states** (`nav-ink`, `nav-muted`, `nav-border`, `nav-hover`): cream copy, pale-green inactive/supporting text, translucent footer/control seams and a cream-tinted hover fill. Source ink and muted aliases resolve to `pine-ink` and `pine-secondary`; the sidebar has no outer right border.

**The Green Field Rule.** Pine green owns the public hero, workspace navigation and specific functional fields such as balance and configuration; keep general reading and data surfaces neutral.

**The Pine Navigation Rule.** Keep the sidebar pine with cream selection and keyboard focus, pale-green secondary text and no outer right border; navigation aliases reuse the pine family without changing billing fields.

**Cascade boundary:** `LandingPage.tsx` imports `landing.css`; `src/main.tsx` then imports `src/styles.css`. The equal-specificity `.relay-app` declarations win over `.landing-home` for `--bg`, `--surface`, `--subtle`, `--text`, `--secondary` and `--border`. `.relay-app` also supplies `--accent`; `brand-tokens.css` supplies `--accent-ink`, `--accent-hover` and `--control-border`. Only the landing supplies the `--hero-*` colors. Thus dark page/surface are the charcoal values in the frontmatter, not the earlier green-tinted declarations in `landing.css`. This is an explicit record of the shipped cascade, not a normalization of either stylesheet.

**Hosted cascade:** `HostedApp.tsx` imports `hosted.css`, `desktop.css`, `workspace-theme.css`, then `workspace-content.css`. The theme layer owns shell/material/control rules; the final content layer owns the recomposed pages, data hierarchy, forms and shared overlay bodies. Imported Desktop styles remain implementation dependencies, not authority to preserve the old page compositions. Base semantic colors still come from `src/styles.css`. The sidebar locally maps `--relay-sidebar` to `--nav-bg`, text to navigation ink, secondary/muted to navigation muted, and `--relay-border` to navigation border without recoloring content. `RequestDetails.tsx` imports `request-details.css`, which owns the receipt composition, token columns, parameters and technical disclosure; obsolete request-specific content-layer overrides are removed. Shared theme rules still supply sentence-case dialog titles, overlay material and close-control sizing. `src/main.tsx` loads `entry.css` after common styles for the standalone entry screen. Do not canonize overridden Inter, hard shadows or old dialog typography; the current compact joined summary is an intentional Hosted composition, not a legacy Desktop layout.

## Typography

**Display and Body Font:** Golos Text, followed by Segoe UI and generic sans-serif. Shared `src/styles/fonts.css`, imported by `brand-tokens.css`, serves `/fonts/GolosText-variable.ttf` (`400–900`, normal, `font-display: swap`); provenance and SIL OFL licensing remain in `public/fonts/README.md` and its adjacent license. Unsupported glyphs, including Chinese where needed, use the stack's fallback; no separate CJK face is bundled. Workspace code and keyboard text retain Cascadia Code / Consolas / monospace.

The hierarchy is warm, restrained and sentence-based. Landing display and headings use medium variable weight (550), balanced wrapping and tight negative tracking, not uppercase kickers. Keep this display ramp confined to the landing:

- **Display:** frontmatter `display`; the second line is smaller (`.79em`) with a 10px separation. At 700px and below it becomes `clamp(40px, 9.2vw, 64px)` / `1.08` / `-.035em`; the second line uses `.88em`, 12px separation and `12ch` maximum width. At 360px and below the main size is 38px.
- **Headline:** frontmatter `headline`; the FAQ heading becomes 40px on phones.
- **Title:** frontmatter `title`; FAQ questions become 18px on phones.
- **Intro:** frontmatter `intro`, at most `45ch`; phones use 15px / 1.7 and `30ch`.
- **Body:** frontmatter `body`, at most `65ch`; phone answers use 14px with the same line height.
- **Label:** frontmatter `label`; header controls use 12px on phones. The brand is 27px / 650 / `-.035em`, reducing to 24px at 700px and 21px at 360px.

Hosted uses fixed operating sizes: page heading 28px / 36px, reducing to 24px / 32px at 639px; common panel headings 16px; action labels 13px; visible navigation labels 13px / 450, selected at 600; dialog headings 18px / 26px, reducing to 16px on phones. Navigation buttons declare 12px in `workspace-theme.css`, but their direct label spans retain explicit 13px from structural `shell.css`; the token records the visible label. Entry headings stay 48px / 1.1, reducing to 36px at 700px.

The final content layer defines deliberate data and supporting roles, not a blanket increase of every label:
- **Overview metrics:** 28px / 32px / 550 with `-.025em` tracking, reducing to 26px / 28px at viewport max-width 639px; labels are 12px / 16px. Chart summaries stay 23px / 550, reducing to 20px.
- **Usage totals:** 26px / 32px / 550 with `-.025em` tracking, reducing to 22px / 28px at page-container max-width 800px, then 20px / 26px at viewport max-width 639px. The joined lower row uses 11px / 16px token labels and 14px / 20px / 550 token and speed values; exact cost values and uncertainty hints remain intact.
- **Request receipt:** model heading 22px / 1.4 / 600 and exact charge 22px / 28px / 600, both reducing to 20px at viewport max-width 639px. The three timing values remain quieter at 18px / 28px / 500. The left-aligned token total uses 18px / 28px / 600; these roles use `-.02em` tracking, not the landing display ramp.
- **Balance:** 40px / 1.2 / 500, reducing to 36px; key/customer titles use 19px.
- **Tables and notes:** column headings 11px / 1.6 / 500, cells 12px / 1.65, supporting notes 12px / 1.7; detail-list values remain 13px and technical IDs retain monospace where appropriate.
- **Empty and session states:** empty headings 32px / 1.2 / 500, reducing to 26px; branded signed-out/not-found headings 40px / 1.15 / 500, reducing to 32px. These are operating states, not the landing display ramp.

**The One Web Voice Rule.** Use the local Golos Text stack across Hosted web surfaces, with distinct public and operating ramps; Desktop remains outside this system.

The effective inherited base is the frontmatter `base`: later `.relay-app` wins over the earlier `.landing-home` declarations of 16px / 1.55. Explicit component sizes and line heights remain authoritative.

## Iconography

Hosted functional icons are authored in `src/components/Icons.tsx`; the shared
`icons.css` owns their geometry and sizing. Use one 20 × 20 grid with rounded
joins/caps, `currentColor`, no silhouette fill and a 1.5px non-scaling stroke.
Small solid points are part of the glyph, not a second decorative colour.
Navigation and utility icons are 20px; standard actions and section icons 18px;
inline metadata, metrics and status icons 16px; dropdown chevrons 14px and
selection checks 16px. Keep both dimensions equal and preserve control targets.

The model catalog uses the same four-part symbol in navigation and Overview;
request traffic uses opposed arrows rather than a medical activity line.
Success, failure and waiting retain distinct check/cross/clock shapes and their
existing accessible labels. The unread bell uses a filled silhouette in the
existing amber (`#986900` light / `#f2c34f` dark), without a separate dot. It gives
a brief 0.9-second nudge once per 45 seconds and rests otherwise; reading restores
the static outline. Reduced motion preserves the fill without movement. Decorative glyphs are hidden
from assistive technology, while a standalone labelled glyph has `role="img"`.
Use semantic `data-icon` values in checks instead of third-party class names.

The landing shares these functional icons. The Relay mark, woven illustrations,
chart geometry and language flags keep their own artwork, grids and semantics;
they are not rescaled as interface icons. Desktop and backend remain unchanged.

## Layout

### Public Landing

The public page retains its original reading column: a 1328px maximum through `--landing-frame-max` and 56px viewport gutters through `--landing-frame-gutter`. The green hero's outer edges align with the visible logo and FAQ heading on the left, and the cabinet action and FAQ rules on the right; do not widen the header or FAQ to match the hero. Header, main and footer share that column, with no extra inset on `.landing-container`. Only hero copy and connection labels have a separate 56px inner inset through `--landing-hero-gutter`. The footer divider ends at the same column edges. This edge alignment persists on phones. The sticky header is at least 88px high; hero top content padding remains 48px. The `clamp(560px, calc(100svh - 112px), 760px)` minimum height keeps the connection labels visible on short desktop screens; content can still grow naturally. The intro uses pretty wrapping and names models, API keys and usage costs without repeating the headline. FAQ columns use `.85fr / 1.15fr`, a 96px gap and 112px / 104px vertical padding. Spacing tokens record recurring steps, not a requirement to snap every measured value to a scale.

- **Do** use the effective light/dark cascade, local font assets and English-first localization across all Hosted surfaces, with separate semantic-key JSON dictionaries and cached on-demand language loading.
